H. Legal review flags

`LEGAL_REVIEW_RECOMMENDED`

1. Marketing image generator / Article 50(4) [media]. This system generates or manipulates media. Article 50(4) turns on whether the output resembles existing persons, objects, places, entities or events and would falsely appear authentic or truthful. The first element was not established, so the duty cannot be ruled out.

1. Embedded vendor chatbot / Article 50(1). Article 50(1) attaches to the provider of the system. The provider relationship for this system is not established, so the duty cannot be assigned.

1. Embedded vendor chatbot / Article 50(2). Marking of synthetic output is a provider obligation, and the provider relationship for this system is not established.

1. Embedded vendor chatbot / Article 50(3). Whether the system performs emotion recognition or biometric categorisation was not established, and it is what triggers this duty.

1. Embedded vendor chatbot / Article 50(4) [text]. This system generates text. Whether any of it is published to inform the public on matters of public interest was not established, and that is what decides the duty.

These are listed, not resolved. Resolving them is legal work, and this assessment does not do legal work.
